# ICLR 2026 Conference Schedule

**TL;DR:** ICLR 2026 took place April 22–27, 2026, in São Paulo, Brazil, featuring three days of main conference sessions (Thursday–Saturday) followed by two days of workshops (Sunday–Monday).

The main conference days featured six invited speakers spanning robotics, generative modeling, computational imaging, developmental science, computer vision, and AI policy. Thursday opened with **Maja Matarić** and **Max Welling**; Friday featured **Katherine Bouman**; and Saturday concluded with **Karen E. Adolph**, **Pablo Arbeláez**, and a Saturday-evening talk by **Percy Liang**.

Each main-conference day was organized around 12 parallel oral session tracks, accompanied by poster sessions, expo talk panels, mentorship sessions, and social events including an opening reception on Thursday evening and a conference dinner on Friday. This structure allowed broad coverage of topics in deep learning, optimization, representation learning, reinforcement learning, natural language processing, and more.

The workshop program spanned Sunday and Monday, hosting 40 workshops in total—20 per day. Workshop topics ranged across reinforcement learning, large language models, scientific discovery, optimization theory, fairness and safety, climate and sustainability, health and biology, and many other areas at the frontier of machine learning research.
