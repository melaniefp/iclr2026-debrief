# Learning Mechanics Blog

[learningmechanics.pub](https://learningmechanics.pub/) is a research blog on the science and theory of deep learning, announced by Jeremy Cohen at his [Sci4DL invited talk (ICLR 2026)](edge-of-stability-cohen-talk.md). The blog publishes in-depth, research-level posts that prioritize foundational understanding over algorithmic contributions.

## Key posts

| Post | Authors | Topic |
|------|---------|-------|
| [Deep linear networks as toy models](https://learningmechanics.pub/deep-linear-nets/) | (editorial) | Exact solutions for training dynamics; sequential rank-incrementing learning; connections to word2vec and LoRA |
| [On neural scaling and the quanta hypothesis](https://learningmechanics.pub/quanta/) | Eric J. Michaud | Proposes that smooth power-law scaling emerges from discrete "quanta" of learning; connects scaling laws to mechanistic interpretability |
| [Perspectives on the science of deep learning](https://learningmechanics.pub/perspectives/) | Jamie Simon, Dhruva Karkada, Florentin Guth | Three essays on methodology for building deep learning theory |

## Significance

The blog represents a growing community effort to establish deep learning theory as a mature empirical science. Its philosophy aligns with Cohen's concluding message at Sci4DL: the field should invest in understanding foundations now, trusting that true understanding will eventually yield practical algorithmic gains. A forthcoming post on "progressive sharpening and the edge of stability" will directly extend the topics from Cohen's talk.
