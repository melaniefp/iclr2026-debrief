# Mechanistic Interpretability

Mechanistic interpretability aims to reverse-engineer the internal computations of neural networks by identifying interpretable features, circuits, and algorithms within trained models. At Sci4DL (ICLR 2026), David Bau's invited talk "Reading Science Back Out of AI" explored how to extract scientific understanding from AI models through careful analysis of their internal representations. This approach treats neural networks as objects of scientific study rather than black boxes.

## Key ideas

- Identifies interpretable features and circuits inside trained neural networks
- Treats models as scientific objects to be studied, not just engineering artifacts
- Internal representations can encode meaningful, recoverable scientific structure
- Complements black-box evaluation with white-box analysis of model internals
