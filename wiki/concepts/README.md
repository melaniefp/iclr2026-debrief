# Wiki — Concepts

Concept articles that define and explain key ideas, methods, and topics in
ML/AI research. Each concept page serves as a hub linking to related papers,
raw captures, and other concepts.

See [wiki/README.md](../README.md) for conventions and frontmatter schema.

## Concept index

- [Affective Computing](affective-computing.md) — Emotion- and affect-aware AI
  systems for social interaction and perception.
- [Concept Bottleneck Models](concept-bottleneck-models.md) — Models that route
  prediction or generation through human-interpretable concepts.
- [Concept Representation Learning](concept-representation-learning.md) — Methods
  for learning robust, meaningful, and intervenable concepts from data.
- [Data Contamination](data-contamination.md) — Benchmark leakage and memorized
  evaluation content in model training pipelines.
- [Edge of Stability](edge-of-stability.md) — Training dynamics near the sharpness
  threshold where gradient descent self-stabilizes.
- [Human-Robot Interaction](human-robot-interaction.md) — How people collaborate
  with, interpret, and adapt to embodied robotic systems.
- [Inference-Time Scaling](inference-time-scaling.md) — Using extra test-time
  compute, search, or verification to improve outputs.
- [LLM Evaluation](llm-evaluation.md) — Measuring model capability, reliability,
  alignment, and benchmark validity.
- [Marin Project](marin-project.md) — Open development of a frontier model and
  its surrounding research process.
- [Mechanistic Interpretability](mechanistic-interpretability.md) —
  Reverse-engineering the circuits and internal computations of models.
- [Model Collapse](model-collapse.md) — Distribution narrowing and forgetting in
  recursive synthetic-data loops.
- [Multi-Turn Conversation](multi-turn-conversation.md) — Reliability challenges
  when tasks are specified gradually across dialogue turns.
- [Neural Scaling Laws](neural-scaling-laws.md) — Power-law relations among
  compute, data, model size, and performance.
- [Open-Source AI](open-source-ai.md) — The spectrum from open weights to fully
  open development in frontier AI.
- [Reward Hacking](reward-hacking.md) — Exploiting proxy rewards, judges, or
  verifiers instead of solving the intended task.
- [Self-Consuming AI](self-consuming-ai.md) — Recursive training and evaluation
  on model-generated content.
- [Socially Assistive Robotics](socially-assistive-robotics.md) — Robots that use
  social interaction to support human wellbeing and independence.
- [Uncertainty in LLMs](uncertainty-in-llms.md) — Ambiguity, knowledge gaps, and
  calibration problems in language-model outputs.
- [Value Alignment](value-alignment.md) — Measuring and shaping how models relate
  to human values and normative constraints.
- [World Models](world-models.md) — Learned predictive models of environments for
  planning, control, and embodied reasoning.
