## Workshops

### 1. AI's Dual-Use Nature

Machine learning technologies adapted for military purposes, potentially without researchers' knowledge or consent. Examining heightened risks associated with particular areas and systems of research, and what it looks like to engage productively in R&D that considers ethics and international law at its core.

### 2. Algorithmic Fairness Across Alignment Procedures and Agentic Systems (AFAA)

AI has transitioned from predictive models to interactive, autonomous agents capable of reasoning, planning, and executing complex goals. Traditional algorithmic fairness frameworks developed primarily for prediction-based decision-making no longer suffice. AFAA is a timely forum for rethinking fairness in AI alignment processes and agentic system development, bridging the gap between rapid technical advances in model capabilities and needed advances in algorithmic fairness frameworks.

### 3. Human Cognitive Science and AI (HC-AIR)

Bridging human cognitive science and AI by bringing together researchers on computational models of human cognition, neurosymbolic AI, human-AI interaction, and cognitively-inspired ML. Exploring how explicit models of human knowledge, cognitive capabilities, and mental states can be integrated into AI reasoning processes. Approaches combining neural and symbolic methods, human causal reasoning patterns, and human teaching signals.

Website: [hc-air.github.io/hcair26](https://hc-air.github.io/hcair26)

### 4. AI & PDE (AI&PDE-ICLR-2026)

PDEs are foundational to modeling complex phenomena across natural sciences and engineering. The workshop convenes researchers from ML, applied mathematics, physics, and engineering to explore AI-driven PDE modeling. Goals: (1) roadmap toward foundation models for PDEs, (2) next-generation representations and architectures, (3) globally inclusive community. Features full papers track and tiny papers track.

### 5. Post-AGI Science and Society

What comes next if AGI becomes ubiquitous, reliable, and affordable? Topics include automated hypothesis generation, causal reasoning in AGI, collaborative discovery, epistemic alignment between humans and machines, and socio-economic shifts driven by pervasive intelligence. Bringing together researchers from ML, philosophy of science, and policy.

### 6. Geometry-Grounded Representation and Generation (GRaM)

Centered on the principle of grounding in geometry: approaches that respect the geometric structure of the problem domain and support geometric reasoning. Theme: scale and simplicity. Exploring when geometric grounding remains necessary, how to effectively scale geometric approaches, and when geometric constraints can be relaxed in favor of simpler alternatives.

### 7. Science of Deep Learning

Promoting a complementary approach to understanding deep learning centered on the scientific method: forming hypotheses and designing controlled experiments to test them. Empirical analyses of deep networks that can validate or falsify existing theories, or answer questions about success or failure of these models.

### 8. AI with Recursive Self-Improvement

RSI is moving from thought experiments to deployed AI systems. LLM agents now rewrite their own codebases or prompts, scientific discovery pipelines schedule continual fine-tuning. Examining algorithms for self-improvement across experience learning, synthetic data pipelines, multimodal agentic systems, weak-to-strong generalization, and inference-time scaling. Organized around five lenses: change targets, temporal regime, mechanisms, operating contexts, and evidence of improvement.

### 9. Associative Memory

Strengthening analytical foundations of associative memory while exploring its emerging role in agentic AI systems. Bringing together researchers from optimization and deep learning, statistical physics, neuroscience and ML systems. Building a cohesive community at the intersection of rigorous mathematical foundations with scalable architectures and applications.

### 10. Logical Reasoning of LLMs

Exploring challenges and opportunities for improving deduction, induction, and abduction capabilities of LLMs, implementing symbolic representation and reasoning, avoiding logical contradictions across responses to multiple related questions, enhancing LLM reasoning by leveraging external logical solvers, and benchmarking logical reasoning and consistencies.

### 11. AI for Accelerated Materials Discovery (AI4Mat-ICLR-2026)

Exploring automated discovery of advanced materials through three pillars: AI-Guided Design, Automated Synthesis, Automated Characterization. Two leading topics: (1) RL & Beyond: The Role of Feedback in AI for Materials Science; (2) Cross-Modal, Unified Materials Representations. Includes Tiny Papers track, travel grants, and dedicated journal venue.

### 12. Safety, Security, and Trustworthiness of Agents in the Wild

AI agents are rapidly deployed in critical applications, yet their unique safety/security challenges remain underexplored. Unlike standard settings, agents act autonomously and make irreversible real-world decisions. First dedicated venue for addressing safety, security, and trustworthiness of agents in the wild.

### 13. Next-X Prediction: Unifying Paradigms for Multimodal Foundation Models

Connecting emerging paradigms — next-token generation, predictive encoding, and diffusion-based modeling — and exploring integration into unified multimodal systems. Key questions: Which paradigm scales most effectively? How do they differ in representation quality, efficiency, and controllability? Can hybrid models combine their strengths?

### 14. Time Series in the Age of Large Models

Foundation models for time series; agents, interpretability, and context-informed predictions. Building on NeurIPS 2024 workshop (99 submissions, 500+ participants) and ICML 2025 Workshop on Foundation Models for Structured Data. Organizers led development of Lag-Llama, Chronos, Moment, Moirai, TimesFM, MLZero, TimeSeriesGym, and evaluation frameworks.

### 15. VerifAI@ICLR'26

Intersection of scale-driven generative AI and correctness-focused verification. Special theme: Building verifiable tasks and environments for RL. Continuing from ICLR 2025 edition focused on LLMs for Code Generation.

### 16. Concepts in NeSy, XAI, and CRL

Convergence around learning high-quality concepts from data across neuro-symbolic integration (NeSy), Explainable AI (XAI), and Causal Representation Learning (CRL). Bringing together researchers interested in learning robust, semantically meaningful concepts. Promoting cross-fertilization across currently independent research tracks.

### 17. AI for Mechanism Design and Strategic Decision Making

Synergy between ML/foundation models and Mechanism Design (MD) and Strategic Decision Making (SDM). Novel AI applications for MD & SDM and theoretical models for AI-driven methods. Real-world applications and case studies from industry.

### 18. Data for Foundation Models (DATA-FM)

Third edition. Data's role in post-training (RLVR), multimodality and agency (cross-modal corpora, interaction logs), and evaluation (leak-resistant test sets). Persistent challenges in data collection, curation, synthesis, copyright, privacy, and fairness.

### 19. Catch, Adapt, and Operate

Continuous ML monitoring in production. Three themes: (1) sensing drift through statistical and representation-based monitoring, (2) responding through adaptive and self-supervised updates, (3) operating at scale in production pipelines. Connecting theory, systems, and real-world practice.

### 20. Lifelong Agents

AI systems that continuously learn, align, and evolve across their operational lifespan. Integrating continual learning, long-term alignment with human values, and self-improvement under resource constraints. Uniting research from RL, LLMs, alignment, embodied AI and more.
