\paragraph{Future work.} 
We mention the challenge of developing an automatic tool for analyzing, 
verifying, and explaining transformers. More broadly, this is an important 
problem for explainable AI, as thoroughly described in the survey 
\citep{survey-explainable}. In particular, lots of practical advances have been 
made on verifying feed-forward neural networks (but not transformers) and some 
practical tools have 
been developed in the last decade (see also the results of the most recent 
annual VNN competition \citep{vnn-result24}). Despite the rather high complexity
($\EXPSPACE$-complete), we pose as a challenge to exploit techniques from 
automated verification \citep{model-checking-book} (e.g. symbolic techniques, 
simulation, etc.) to verify transformers in practice.
