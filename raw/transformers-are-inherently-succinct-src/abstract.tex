\begin{abstract}
    We propose succinctness as a measure of the expressive power
    of a transformer in describing a concept. To this end, we prove that 
    transformers are highly expressive in that they can represent formal 
    languages substantially more succinctly than standard representations of 
    formal languages like finite automata and Linear Temporal Logic (LTL) 
    formulas. As a by-product of this expressivity, we show that verifying 
    properties of transformers is provably intractable (i.e.  
    EXPSPACE-complete).
